Who this notice applies to

This privacy notice applies to clients of Hidden InSight Labs — individuals and organizations who engage us for software evaluation, UX testing, or programme management advisory services. It explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data in accordance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).

Section 01

Who we are

Hidden InSight Labs is the trading name of System Lens Evaluation (システムレンズ評価), a sole proprietorship operated from Japan providing software evaluation, UX testing, and programme management services to B2B clients. We are the party responsible for handling the personal data we hold about our clients and their representatives.

Section 05

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary, and only with third parties bound to protect it appropriately:

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted third-party tools to operate our business. These providers process data only as we instruct:

  • Email & communication — Proton Mail AG (Switzerland; end-to-end encrypted)
  • File storage — Dropbox, Inc. and Google LLC (United States). Client files and internal working documents are stored here.
  • Website hosting — GitHub, Inc. / GitHub Pages (United States). Static site files are hosted via GitHub; no personal data is processed by GitHub beyond standard server logs.
  • Transactional email — Resend, Inc. (United States). Enquiry submissions are transmitted to us through Resend's email delivery infrastructure and are not retained by Resend beyond their standard log retention period.
  • Serverless compute — Cloudflare, Inc. (United States). Contact form submissions are processed by a Cloudflare Worker before being passed to Resend.
  • Website analytics — Google Analytics 4 / Google LLC (United States). See Section 10 for full details.
  • Payments — Stripe, Inc. (United States) and Wise (United Kingdom), for processing payments and settlements.

Professional advisers

Legal, tax, or accounting advisers where necessary for compliance or dispute resolution, bound by professional confidentiality obligations.

Legal & regulatory authorities

Japanese tax authorities or other authorities where we are legally required to disclose information.

We will not share your data with any other party without notifying you first, except where required by law.

Section 06

Cross-border transfers

As set out in Section 5, several of our service providers are based outside Japan, principally in the United States. Under APPI, transferring personal data to a third party located in a foreign country generally requires one of the following: the destination country having a data protection framework recognized by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC) as equivalent, appropriate contractual or organizational safeguards with the recipient, or your consent to the transfer.

By engaging our services, you acknowledge and consent to your personal data being processed by the service providers listed in Section 5, including providers located in the United States, for the purposes described in this notice. We select providers that maintain their own data protection commitments (such as encryption in transit and at rest, and restricted access) and we review these arrangements periodically.

You may request details of the specific safeguards in place for any particular service by contacting us using the details in Section 12.

Section 08

Your rights

Under APPI, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Disclosure — request disclosure of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct, add to, or delete inaccurate data about you
  • Suspension of use — ask us to suspend use of your data in certain circumstances, including where it was obtained improperly or is no longer needed for its stated purpose
  • Objection to third-party provision — object to your data being provided to third parties beyond what is described in this notice

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe, generally within two weeks. We will not charge a fee for exercising your rights in most cases. If a request is clearly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or decline the request, and will explain why.

Section 09

Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, or destruction. Measures include:

  • End-to-end encrypted email (Proton Mail) for sensitive communications
  • Strong password policies and multi-factor authentication on all accounts holding client data
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Encrypted file storage for sensitive documents
  • Regular review of our security practices

In the event of a personal data breach likely to harm your rights or interests, we will notify Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission as required by law and, where appropriate, inform you directly without undue delay.

Section 10

Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymized data about how visitors use the site — including pages visited, time on page, and general geographic region (country level). This helps us understand which content is useful and improve the site.

GA4 sets cookies on your device to distinguish visitors and sessions. The data collected is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Opt out: you can prevent GA4 from collecting data about your visit by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using a browser privacy extension that blocks analytics trackers.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or any tracking technologies beyond GA4. Your browser may also store technically necessary information (such as session state) as part of normal website operation.
