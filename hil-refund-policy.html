Nature of Service

Hidden InSight Labs provides digital software evaluation services. All deliverables — including findings reports, recommendations, and walkthrough meetings — are delivered digitally. No physical goods are shipped at any time.

Because our services involve the allocation of independent tester time and analyst resources immediately upon engagement commencement, our refund policy reflects the nature of this work.

Level 1 (New Client Evaluation) — Refund Terms

Before testing begins: full refund available if requested before access to your app has been confirmed and testing has commenced.

After testing begins: no refund is available once access to your app has been confirmed and testing has commenced. Tester time and resources have been committed at this point.

Specifically:

  • Cancellation before onboarding instructions are sent — full refund, no questions asked. Contact us within 24 hours of purchase at contact@hiddeninsightlabs.com.
  • Cancellation after onboarding instructions are sent but before app access is confirmed — full refund available. Please contact us as soon as possible.
  • Cancellation after app access has been confirmed and testing has commenced — no refund is available. The engagement is considered underway.
  • If we are unable to complete the evaluation due to a failure on our part (for example, inability to access the app despite valid credentials being provided) — a full refund will be issued.

Report Quality

We are committed to delivering findings reports that are structured, specific, and actionable. If you believe the report delivered does not meet a reasonable standard of quality for the scope tested, please contact us at contact@hiddeninsightlabs.com within 7 days of delivery.

We will review your concern and, at our discretion, offer one of the following resolutions: supplementary findings, a revised report section, or a credit toward a future engagement. Refunds on quality grounds are considered case by case and are not automatic.

Level 2 through Level 5, and Custom Engagements

For engagements beyond Level 1, refund and cancellation terms are governed by the specific service agreement or statement of work entered into between the client and Hidden InSight Labs prior to engagement commencement. If no separate agreement is in place, the following applies:

  • Cancellations made before work has commenced — full refund of any deposit paid
  • Cancellations made after work has commenced — refund of any portion not yet expended on tester time and resources, at our reasonable assessment

Please contact contact@hiddeninsightlabs.com to discuss cancellation of any ongoing engagement.

Add-Ons (Session Recordings & Focus Groups)

Add-on services (raw session recordings and hosted focus groups) follow the same refund terms as the primary engagement level to which they are attached. Once a session recording or focus group has commenced, no refund is available for that add-on.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or discuss a cancellation, email contact@hiddeninsightlabs.com with:

  • Your name and company
  • The email address used at purchase
  • Your Stripe receipt or order reference
  • The reason for your request

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed via the original payment method through Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
