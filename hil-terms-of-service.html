Engagement Levels & Scope

Hidden InSight Labs offers independent software evaluation across five fixed levels of scope and rigor (Level 1 through Level 5), plus Custom engagements scoped individually. Every level produces a documented Real-World Validation Report and includes a technical meeting to walk you through what we found.

Level 1 — New Client Evaluation ($300). A fixed-scope, introductory engagement available once per organization. It includes:

  • Evaluation of one end-to-end user flow within your app or software product, as specified by you at the time of booking
  • Testing conducted by a small, focused panel of independent human testers
  • A written Real-World Validation Report, including prioritized findings and severity ratings
  • One results walkthrough meeting, scheduled within 7 days of report delivery

Level 1 does not include automated testing, code review, re-testing after fixes, multiple flows, or any deliverable not listed above. Broader or repeat coverage is available via Level 2 and above.
