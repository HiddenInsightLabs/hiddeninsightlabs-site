Frequently asked questions

What exactly does "one user flow" mean? ▼
A single end-to-end journey through your app — for example, the entire sign-up process, or adding an item to a cart and completing checkout, or creating a user profile. We test that complete journey from start to finish. If you're unsure which flow to choose, we'll advise you during onboarding.
How do I give you access to my app? ▼
After purchase you'll receive onboarding instructions. For iOS apps, a TestFlight link works best. For Android, a Play Store beta invite or direct APK. For web apps, a URL plus any login credentials needed. We handle everything through email — no repository access or SDK required.
When does the 48-hour clock start? ▼
The 48-hour delivery window starts once we've confirmed both your payment and received everything we need to access and test your app. If access information takes time to prepare, that's fine — the clock doesn't start until we have what we need.
What if I need more than one flow tested? ▼
The Pilot covers one flow. If you need broader coverage — multiple flows, more testers, or a second testing pass after fixes — our Focused ($3,500) or Standard ($7,000+) tiers are the right fit. We'll be happy to recommend the right tier after your Pilot.
Is this really only available once? ▼
Yes — the $300 price is a one-time introductory offer, available once per company. It's designed so you can see our work before committing to a larger engagement. If you return for further testing, the standard Essentials rate ($1,500) applies.
What if I want to cancel? ▼
If you need to cancel before testing begins, contact us at hello@hiddeninsightlabs.com and we'll do our best to accommodate a cancellation or credit. Once testing has commenced, no refund is issued — see our Refund Policy for full details.
Is my app information kept confidential? ▼
Absolutely. All client information, app access credentials, and findings are strictly confidential. We will not share, publish, or reference your product with any third party without your express written consent. Testers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
